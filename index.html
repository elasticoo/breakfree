Recognize the signs

Warning Signs of Gambling Addiction

If any of these feel familiar, it might be time to seek help. There's no shame in it — only strength.

1

You spend more money gambling than you can afford to lose

2

You chase losses, trying to win back what you've lost

3

You lie to family and friends about your gambling

4

You feel restless or irritable when trying to stop

5

You gamble to escape problems or relieve feelings of helplessness

6

You've jeopardized relationships, jobs, or education because of gambling

7

You borrow money or sell possessions to gamble

8

You feel guilty or anxious after gambling
